Park Layout Overview

🏠 RV Sites

50+ Sites Available

Site Types:

  • Premium Lakefront: Direct lake views, 50 amp, full hookups
  • Standard Full Hookup: 50/30 amp, water, sewer, cable ready
  • Back-in Sites: 30-40 feet, easy access, level pads
  • Pull-through Sites: 35-45 feet, convenient for large RVs

Site Features:

  • Level concrete pads
  • Picnic tables at select sites
  • Fire rings available
  • Additional vehicle parking

🏢 Main Facilities

Central Location

Office & Check-in:

  • Registration and information
  • Camp store supplies
  • WiFi password distribution
  • Local area information

Bathhouse Complex:

  • Men's and women's facilities
  • Private shower stalls
  • ADA accessible
  • Climate controlled

🌊 Lake Access

Direct Access

Water Activities:

  • Swimming Area: Designated safe swimming zone
  • Fishing Access: Shoreline fishing from multiple points
  • Boat Access: Nearby boat ramps and marinas
  • Beach Area: Sandy areas for relaxation

Safety Features:

  • Clearly marked swimming areas
  • Emergency contact information posted
  • Life ring stations
  • Good lighting for evening activities

🐕 Pet Areas

Pet Friendly

Dog Areas:

  • Off-leash Area: Fenced dog run for exercise
  • Walking Trails: Pet-friendly paths throughout park
  • Waste Stations: Cleanup supplies provided
  • Water Stations: Fresh water for pets

Pet Policies:

  • Leash required in common areas
  • 2 pet maximum per site
  • Current vaccinations required
  • Pet fee applies - see rates

Getting Around the Park

🚗 Driving Directions Within Park

From Main Entrance:

  1. Check-in: Stop at office for registration and site assignment
  2. Main Road: Follow paved main road through park
  3. Site Numbers: Look for clearly marked site numbers
  4. Speed Limit: 5 MPH throughout park for safety

Site Access Tips:

  • All roads are paved for easy RV navigation
  • Pull-through sites accessible from main road
  • Back-in sites may require slight maneuvering
  • Staff available to guide large RVs if needed

🚶‍♀️ Walking Paths & Trails

Pedestrian Areas:

  • Lakefront Trail: Scenic walk along the shoreline
  • Main Pathway: Connects all major facilities
  • Pet Walking Areas: Designated paths for dog walking
  • Exercise Loop: Circuit path for morning walks/jogs

Accessibility:

  • ADA-compliant pathways to main facilities
  • Well-lit paths for evening safety
  • Benches and rest areas along trails
  • Clear signage throughout park
