🏕️ Reservations & Booking

You can make a reservation three ways:

  • Online: Use our online booking system at fireflyreservations.com
  • Phone: Call us at 940-277-3435 during office hours
  • Walk-in: Visit our office (subject to availability)

We recommend booking online for the fastest service and best rate availability.

Our cancellation policy varies by season and length of stay:

  • Standard Cancellation: 48 hours before arrival for full refund
  • Peak Season: 7 days advance notice required
  • Holiday Weekends: 14 days advance notice required
  • Monthly Stays: 30 days advance notice required

Cancellation fees may apply for late cancellations. See full policy in your booking confirmation.

Yes, deposits are required for all reservations:

  • Nightly Stays: First night's rent due at booking
  • Weekly Stays: 50% deposit required
  • Monthly Stays: First month's rent plus security deposit
  • Holiday Periods: 100% payment required in advance

Deposits are fully refundable with proper advance notice as per our cancellation policy.

  • Check-in: 2:00 PM or later
  • Check-out: 12:00 PM (noon)
  • Office Hours: Daily 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM
  • Late Arrivals: Please call ahead to arrange

Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request and availability.

Site assignments are handled as follows:

  • Online Bookings: Sites assigned at check-in based on RV size and preferences
  • Specific Requests: Call to request specific sites (not guaranteed)
  • Site Types: Currently offering back-in sites with more options coming soon
  • Upgrades: Available at check-in if sites are available

We do our best to accommodate preferences while ensuring all guests have appropriate sites.

All sites include full hookups:

  • Wi-Fi: Free high-speed internet throughout the park

Yes, Wi-Fi is complimentary for all guests:

Speed may vary during peak usage times. Business-grade connection available for long-term guests.

📋 Policies & Rules

Yes, CampPointe is pet-friendly with some restrictions:

  • Pet Limit: Maximum 2 pets per site
  • Pet Fee: $5 per pet per night (see current rates)
  • Leash Rule: Pets must be on leash in all common areas
  • Vaccinations: Current vaccination records required
  • Cleanup: Owners responsible for waste cleanup
  • Breed Restrictions: Some aggressive breeds subject to approval

We have designated pet areas including an off-leash dog run.

Quiet hours are enforced for everyone's enjoyment:

  • Quiet Hours: 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM daily
  • Noise Level: Keep noise to minimum during quiet hours
  • Generators: No generators during quiet hours
  • Music: No loud music anytime
  • Enforcement: Violations may result in removal

Visitors are welcome with some guidelines:

  • Day Visitors: Welcome 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Visitor Fee: $5 per day visitor
  • Registration: All visitors must check in at office
  • Overnight Guests: Additional fees apply, subject to site capacity
  • Responsibility: Guests responsible for visitor behavior

Campfires are permitted with safety guidelines:

  • Fire Rings: Use designated fire rings only
  • Firewood: Available for purchase at office
  • Burn Bans: No fires during county burn bans
  • Supervision: Never leave fires unattended
  • Hours: Fires must be extinguished by 11:00 PM
  • Safety: Water/sand must be nearby for extinguishing

We can accommodate RVs of various sizes:

  • Maximum Length: Up to 45 feet (including towed vehicle)
  • Pull-through Sites: Available for easier parking
  • Big Rig Friendly: Wide sites with easy maneuvering
  • Site Assignment: Based on your RV specifications
  • Multiple Vehicles: Additional parking available

Please specify your RV length when making reservations.

🎣 Activities & Recreation

Lake Texoma offers world-class fishing opportunities:

  • Striped Bass: Lake Texoma is famous for hybrid striper fishing
  • Other Species: Largemouth bass, catfish, crappie, sand bass
  • Fishing License: Texas or Oklahoma license required
  • Guides: Professional fishing guides available
  • Cleaning Station: Available at CampPointe

Boat and equipment rentals are available at nearby marinas:

  • Grandpappy Point Marina: 10 minutes away
  • Tanglewood Marina: 15 minutes away
  • Available Rentals: Pontoon boats, fishing boats, jet skis
  • Equipment: Kayaks, canoes, fishing gear
  • Reservations: Call marinas directly for availability

Several hiking opportunities are available in the area:

  • Eisenhower State Park: 15 minutes - multiple trails
  • Hagerman National Wildlife Refuge: 25 minutes - nature trails
  • CampPointe Trails: Walking paths around the property
  • Difficulty Levels: Easy to moderate trails available
  • Wildlife Viewing: Excellent bird watching opportunities

Lake Texoma offers many family-friendly activities:

  • Swimming: Designated safe swimming areas
  • Fishing: Great for teaching kids to fish
  • Playgrounds: Available at nearby state parks
  • Beach Time: Sandy areas for building sandcastles
  • Wildlife Watching: Kids love spotting birds and wildlife
  • Boating: Family-friendly pontoon boat rentals
