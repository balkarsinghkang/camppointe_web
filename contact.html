🚗 Driving Directions

From Dallas/Fort Worth Area

  1. Take US-75 North toward Sherman/Denison
  2. Exit at FM 901 (Exit 67) toward Lake Texoma
  3. Turn right (east) on FM 901
  4. Continue for approximately 8 miles
  5. CampPointe will be on your right side

Approximate drive time: 1.5 hours

From Oklahoma City Area

  1. Take I-35 South toward Ardmore
  2. Exit at US-70 West toward Madill/Lake Texoma
  3. Continue on US-70 West for about 15 miles
  4. Turn left (south) on FM 901
  5. CampPointe will be on your left after 3 miles

Approximate drive time: 2 hours

🚛 RV Arrival Tips

  • Road Conditions: FM 901 is RV-friendly with wide shoulders
  • Fuel Stops: Last convenient fuel is in Whitesboro or Gordonville
  • Supplies: Grocery stores available in Sherman (15 minutes away)
  • Check-in: Proceed directly to the office upon arrival
  • Large RVs: Call ahead if your rig is over 40 feet
