Park Policies & Guidelines

Rules and guidelines to ensure everyone enjoys their Lake Texoma experience

General Park Rules

🌙 Quiet Hours

Quiet Time: 10:00 PM - 7:00 AM daily

  • Generator use permitted 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM only
  • Music and television at reasonable volumes
  • Respect your neighbors at all times
  • Children must be supervised during quiet hours

🚗 Vehicle & Parking

Speed Limit: 10 MPH throughout park

  • One RV and one vehicle per site included
  • Additional vehicles: $10/night
  • Parking only in designated areas
  • No washing vehicles at RV sites
  • Motorcycles and ATVs prohibited

🐕 Pet Policy

Pets Welcome: Maximum 2 per site

  • Must be leashed at all times in common areas
  • Owners responsible for cleanup immediately
  • Current vaccination records required
  • Aggressive or disruptive animals not permitted
  • Pet waste stations provided throughout park
  • No pets left unattended

Safety & Security Guidelines

🔥 Fire Safety

  • Fires only in designated fire rings
  • Firewood available for purchase at office
  • No unattended fires - extinguish completely when leaving
  • Follow all county burn bans when in effect
  • Keep fire extinguisher or water nearby
  • No ground fires or fire pits other than provided rings

🏊 Water Safety

  • Swimming and water activities at your own risk
  • Children must be supervised near water at all times
  • Life jackets recommended for all water activities
  • No diving from docks, piers, or shoreline
  • Be aware of changing water levels and conditions
  • Alcohol prohibited while operating watercraft

🚨 Emergency Procedures

  • Emergency services: Call 911 immediately
  • Park office: 940-277-3435 (8 AM - 8 PM)
  • After-hours emergencies: Contact local authorities first
  • Report all accidents to park management
  • First aid kit available at office
  • Nearest hospital: Wilson N. Jones, Sherman (15 minutes)

Facility Usage Policies

🚿 Restrooms & Showers

Our facilities are maintained for everyone's comfort and safety.

  • Keep facilities clean and tidy for all guests
  • Report maintenance issues immediately to office
  • Personal items left overnight will be removed
  • Children under 12 must be accompanied by adult
  • Maximum shower time: 15 minutes during busy periods

🧺 Laundry Facilities

Coin-operated washers and dryers available for guest use.

  • Cost: $3.00 wash, $3.00 dry (exact change required)
  • Maximum time per load: 2 hours
  • Remove clothes promptly when cycle complete
  • Clean lint trap after each dryer use
  • Report machine problems to office immediately

🎣 Recreation Areas

Enjoy our recreational facilities responsibly.

  • Playground: Children must be supervised
  • Fishing areas: Texas fishing license required
  • Boat ramp: Use at your own risk
  • Walking trails: Stay on marked paths
  • Clean up after use and dispose of trash properly

Checkout & Site Condition

📋 Site Condition Requirements

  • Leave site clean and debris-free - Remove all personal items
  • Dispose of trash properly - Use designated dumpster areas
  • Report damages immediately - Honesty helps us maintain quality
  • Return site to original condition - Move picnic tables, etc. back
  • Check for forgotten items - We're not responsible for left items

💰 Checkout Fees & Charges

Standard Checkout Time 12:00 PM - No charge
Late Checkout (after 12 PM) $25.00
Excessive Cleanup Required $50.00 minimum
Damage to Site or Facilities Cost of repair/replacement
Lost or Damaged Keys/Cards $25.00 replacement fee

⚡ Special Circumstances

🌪️ Severe Weather

Full refund when official weather warnings prevent safe travel

  • National Weather Service warnings
  • Road closures due to weather
  • Documentation required

🏥 Medical Emergency

Case-by-case review with proper documentation

  • Hospital admission records
  • Doctor's note for travel restrictions
  • Emergency room documentation

🇺🇸 Military Deployment

Full refund with copy of deployment orders

  • Official deployment orders
  • PCS (Permanent Change of Station) orders
  • Emergency military duty

🏕️ Park Closure

Full refund for park-initiated cancellations

  • Emergency maintenance
  • Natural disasters
  • Government mandates

Guest Code of Conduct

✅ Expected Behavior

  • Respectful Treatment: Be courteous to staff and fellow guests
  • Rule Compliance: Follow all posted park rules and guidelines
  • Responsible Enjoyment: Have fun while being mindful of others
  • Family-Friendly: Help maintain a wholesome atmosphere
  • Environmental Care: Respect nature and wildlife
  • Safety First: Look out for your safety and others'

❌ Prohibited Activities

Noise & Disruption

  • Excessive noise after quiet hours
  • Loud music or television
  • Disruptive behavior
  • Fighting or aggressive behavior

Illegal Activities

  • Illegal drug use or possession
  • Underage drinking
  • Public intoxication
  • Any illegal activities

Commercial Activities

  • Unauthorized vendors or sales
  • Solicitation of guests
  • Commercial photography without permission
  • Business meetings or events

Environmental

  • Littering or improper waste disposal
  • Dumping gray or black water on ground
  • Cutting or damaging vegetation
  • Feeding wildlife

Management Rights & Responsibilities

🏛️ Management Authority

CampPointe management reserves the right to:

  • Refuse Service: Deny accommodations to anyone violating policies
  • Remove Guests: Ask guests to leave for policy violations
  • No Refund: No refund for ejection due to rule violations
  • Policy Changes: Update policies with reasonable notice
  • Site Assignment: Assign and reassign sites as needed
  • Inspection Rights: Inspect sites for safety and compliance

⚖️ Liability & Responsibility

Guest Responsibility: Guests are responsible for their personal property, vehicles, and actions. CampPointe is not liable for:

  • Theft, damage, or loss of personal property
  • Injuries occurring on park premises
  • Weather-related issues or natural disasters
  • Wildlife encounters or interactions
  • Water-related accidents or incidents
  • Vehicle damage from roads or parking areas

Insurance Recommendation: We strongly recommend guests maintain adequate insurance coverage for their RV, personal property, and liability.
