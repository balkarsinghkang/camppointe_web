## Risk Assessment & Mitigation

### Potential Challenges
1. **Wix Platform Limitations**: Some advanced SEO features may be restricted
2. **Competition**: Other RV parks may have established online presence  
3. **Seasonality**: Traffic and bookings may vary significantly by season
4. **Technical Issues**: Wix updates could affect customizations

### Mitigation Strategies
- Focus on Wix-native optimization techniques
- Emphasize unique selling points and superior service
- Create year-round content and off-season promotions
- Document all customizations and have backups
- Maintain regular communication with Wix support
