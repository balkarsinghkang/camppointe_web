Monthly Stay Application Process

CampPointe maintains high standards to ensure a peaceful, safe community for all residents. Our screening process helps us maintain the quiet, family-friendly atmosphere our long-term guests appreciate.

1

Initial Contact & Tour

Call us at 940-277-3435 to discuss availability and schedule a tour. We'll show you available sites and explain our community guidelines.

2

Complete Application

Submit our monthly stay application with references, employment verification, and RV information. We'll review your application within 24-48 hours.

3

Background Screening

Professional background check to ensure community safety and compatibility. We look for responsible tenants who will respect our community standards.

4

Move-In & Orientation

Once approved, pay first month's rent and security deposit. We'll provide orientation on park rules, amenities, and local resources.

Application Requirements

📋 Personal Information

  • Valid government-issued ID
  • Social Security number for background check
  • Previous addresses (last 3 years)
  • Emergency contact information

💼 Financial Verification

  • Proof of income (pay stubs, benefits, etc.)
  • Bank account information
  • Employment verification or retirement docs
  • Income must be 2.5x monthly rent

🚐 RV Information

  • RV registration and insurance
  • RV length, height, and slide-out details
  • Vehicle information for additional cars
  • Pet information (if applicable)

📞 References

  • Previous landlord or RV park references
  • Personal references (non-family)
  • Employment reference
  • Character references preferred

Payment Terms & Deposit Information

💰 Security Deposit

  • Amount: One month's rent
  • Refundable: Yes, with 30-day notice
  • Covers: Damages, early departure
  • Return: Within 15 days of departure

📅 Payment Schedule

  • Due Date: 1st of each month
  • Grace Period: 5 days with $25 late fee
  • Methods: Check, money order, bank transfer
  • Auto-Pay: Available for qualified residents

📄 Lease Terms

  • Initial Term: Month-to-month or 6+ months
  • Notice Required: 30 days to vacate
  • Rate Increases: 60-day notice required
  • Renewal: Automatic month-to-month

🏠 What's Included

  • All Utilities: Electric, water, sewer, trash
  • Internet: High-speed Wi-Fi throughout
  • Amenities: Laundry, bathhouse, lake access
  • Maintenance: Common area and infrastructure

Community Guidelines for Long-Term Residents

Our community thrives because we maintain high standards for cleanliness, safety, and respect. These guidelines help ensure everyone enjoys their stay at CampPointe.

🤝 Community Respect

  • Quiet hours: 10:00 PM - 6:00 AM daily
  • Keep sites clean and well-maintained
  • Respect neighbors' privacy and space
  • Report maintenance issues promptly

🚗 Vehicles & Parking

  • Maximum 2 vehicles per site
  • All vehicles must be registered and insured
  • No inoperable vehicles permitted
  • 5 MPH speed limit throughout park

🐕 Pet Policy

  • Maximum 2 pets per site
  • Pets must be leashed in common areas
  • Pet waste must be cleaned up immediately
  • Current vaccination records required

👥 Visitors & Guests

  • Visitors welcome 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • Register all visitors at office
  • Resident responsible for guest behavior
  • Overnight guests subject to approval
