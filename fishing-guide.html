Complete Lake Texoma Fishing Guide

Expert tips and prime fishing spots from CampPointe's lake access

🎣 Striped Bass Capital 🐟 Year-Round Fishing 🚤 89,000 Acre Lake

Why Lake Texoma is a Fisherman's Paradise

Lake Texoma is renowned as one of America's premier fishing destinations, spanning 89,000 acres across Texas and Oklahoma. From CampPointe RV Park, you have direct access to some of the lake's most productive fishing waters, making it the perfect base for serious anglers and casual fishermen alike.

🏆 Record Fish

  • Striped Bass: 35+ pounds (state record waters)
  • Blue Catfish: 60+ pounds regularly caught
  • White Bass: School runs of 100+ fish
  • Crappie: 2-3 pound slabs common

🎯 Prime Species

  • Striped Bass - World-class fishery
  • Blue & Channel Catfish - Trophy sizes
  • White & Hybrid Bass - Exciting action
  • Crappie - Excellent numbers
  • Largemouth Bass - Quality fish

📅 Best Seasons

  • Spring: Spawning runs, active fish
  • Summer: Deep water structure
  • Fall: Feeding frenzy season
  • Winter: Trophy catfish time

🐟 Striped Bass Fishing - Lake Texoma's Crown Jewel

Lake Texoma is famous worldwide for its striped bass population. These powerful fish provide some of the most exciting freshwater fishing experiences in North America, with fish regularly exceeding 20 pounds and occasional monsters topping 30+.

🎯 Prime Locations from CampPointe Area

Near Park Waters

  • Creek channels - Follow depth changes
  • Points and humps - Structure fishing
  • Tributary mouths - Bait fish concentrations
  • Rocky shorelines - Early morning action

Popular Striper Spots

  • Washita River arm - Consistent producers
  • Red River arm - Deep water structure
  • Main lake points - Open water schools
  • Dam area - Current and depth

🎣 Proven Techniques & Baits

Live Bait Fishing

  • Fresh shad - #1 producer year-round
  • Threadfin shad - Match the hatch
  • Live perch - Trophy fish magnet
  • Presentation: Free-line or Carolina rig

Artificial Lures

  • Slab spoons - Vertical jigging deep schools
  • Alabama rigs - Multiple bait presentation
  • Topwater - Dawn and dusk surface action
  • Deep diving cranks - Trolling main lake

📅 Seasonal Striped Bass Patterns

🌸 Spring (March-May)

  • Pre-spawn: Shallow flats and creek mouths
  • Spawn: River arms and tributaries
  • Post-spawn: Transition back to main lake
  • Best baits: Live shad, large swimbaits

☀️ Summer (June-August)

  • Deep water: 30-50 foot depths
  • Thermocline: Suspended in oxygen layer
  • Early/late: Surface feeding at dawn/dusk
  • Best baits: Downrigger shad, deep spoons

🍂 Fall (September-November)

  • Feeding frenzy: Building winter reserves
  • Surface action: Spectacular topwater
  • School behavior: Large aggressive groups
  • Best baits: Topwater, live bait

❄️ Winter (December-February)

  • Deep structure: Channel breaks and humps
  • Slow presentation: Vertical jigging
  • Quality fish: Trophy potential high
  • Best baits: Live shad, slow spoons

🐾 Catfish Capital - Blues & Channels

Lake Texoma produces some of the largest blue catfish in the country, with fish over 50 pounds caught regularly. The combination of deep water, abundant forage, and excellent habitat creates ideal conditions for trophy catfish.

🎯 Trophy Blue Catfish

Prime Locations

  • River channels: Deep holes with current
  • Creek bends: Outside curves with depth
  • Humps and ridges: Underwater structure
  • Dam tailwaters: Current and oxygen

Best Baits for Blues

  • Fresh shad: Cut or whole for big fish
  • Live perch: Trophy catfish candy
  • Chicken liver: Classic producer
  • Prepared baits: Punch and dip baits

🐱 Channel Catfish Action

Eating Size Fish

  • Shallow flats: 8-15 feet near structure
  • Creek channels: Edge drops and ledges
  • Rocky areas: Points and riprap
  • Night fishing: Move shallow to feed

Channel Cat Techniques

  • Bottom rigs: Carolina and slip sinker
  • Multi-rod setup: Cover more water
  • Fresh bait: Change every 30 minutes
  • Patience: Let them take the bait

⏰ Best Catfishing Times

🌙 Night Fishing

Peak activity from sunset to midnight. Catfish move shallow to feed under cover of darkness.

🌅 Early Morning

Dawn feeding continues from night activity. Great time for fresh bait presentation.

🌧️ Weather Changes

Before storms and pressure changes trigger feeding. Overcast days excellent.

❄️ Winter Patterns

Cold weather concentrates fish in deep holes. Slow presentations work best.

🎣 White Bass & Crappie - Action Fishing

⚡ White Bass - School Fish Excitement

Finding Schools

  • Electronics: Use fish finder to locate
  • Birds: Seagulls indicate surface feeding
  • Boils: Water disturbance from feeding
  • Points and flats: Traditional holding areas

Effective Lures

  • Small spoons: 1/4 to 1/2 ounce silver
  • Jigs: White or chartreuse hair jigs
  • Spinners: Inline and safety pin
  • Live minnows: Small shiners or shad

🗓️ White Bass Seasons

  • Spring run: March-April tributaries spawn
  • Summer schools: Main lake points and humps
  • Fall feeding: September-October best action
  • Winter patterns: Deep water structure

🐟 Crappie - Slab Fishing

Structure Fishing

  • Brush piles: Submerged cover
  • Standing timber: Flooded trees
  • Creek channels: Depth changes
  • Docks and piers: Man-made structure

Crappie Baits

  • Minnows: Live on jig heads
  • Tube jigs: Various colors
  • Hair jigs: Marabou and bucktail
  • Crankbaits: Small diving plugs

🎯 Prime Crappie Times

  • Pre-spawn: February-March shallow movement
  • Spawn period: April spawning in 2-8 feet
  • Summer pattern: Deep structure 15-25 feet
  • Fall schooling: October feeding up

📋 Fishing Regulations & Guidelines

🎫 Fishing License Requirements

Texas Residents

  • Texas fishing license required
  • Age requirements: 17+ need license
  • Where to buy: Online, tackle shops, Walmart
  • Special stamps: None required for Lake Texoma

Oklahoma Residents

  • Oklahoma fishing license valid
  • Reciprocal waters: Both states honored
  • Age requirements: 16+ need license
  • Multi-state option: Buy Texas license too

Out-of-State Visitors

  • Non-resident license required
  • Duration options: Daily, 5-day, annual
  • Online purchase: Available 24/7
  • Temporary license: Print at home

🌱 Conservation & Best Practices

Catch & Release

  • Handle carefully - Support fish properly
  • Quick photos - Minimize air exposure
  • Use circle hooks - Reduce deep hooking
  • Revive properly - Move fish forward gently

Keep What You Eat

  • Clean immediately - Best quality fish
  • Ice quickly - Maintain freshness
  • Use whole fish - Minimize waste
  • Share the bounty - Give extras away
