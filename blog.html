Complete Guide to Lake Texoma Fishing

February 1, 2026 By CampPointe Staff

Lake Texoma is renowned as one of the premier fishing destinations in the South, particularly famous for its exceptional striped bass population. Whether you're a seasoned angler or just starting out, this comprehensive guide will help you make the most of your fishing experience.

🎣 Fish Species at Lake Texoma

  • Striped Bass (Hybrid): The star attraction - Lake Texoma holds multiple state records
  • Largemouth Bass: Excellent throughout the year, especially in spring
  • Catfish: Blue and channel catfish abundant, great for family fishing
  • Crappie: Spring runs are spectacular, perfect for beginners
  • Sand Bass: Fun to catch and great for kids

🗓️ Best Fishing Seasons

  • Spring (March-May): Crappie spawning, excellent bass fishing
  • Summer (June-August): Early morning and evening striper fishing
  • Fall (September-November): Peak striper season, cooler weather
  • Winter (December-February): Best striper fishing of the year

📍 Top Fishing Spots (Near CampPointe)

  • Big Mineral Creek: 5 minutes - excellent for stripers and bass
  • Little Mineral Creek: 8 minutes - protected coves, great for crappie
  • Main Lake Points: 10 minutes - deep water striper fishing
  • Denison Dam Area: 20 minutes - trophy striper hotspot

🎯 Fishing Tips for Success

  • Best Times: Early morning (6-9 AM) and evening (6-8 PM)
  • Equipment: Medium-heavy rods, 15-20 lb test line for stripers
  • Bait: Live shad, spoons, and topwater lures work best
  • Weather: Overcast days often produce better fishing

Pro Tip: CampPointe has a fish cleaning station available for guests. Don't forget to get your Texas or Oklahoma fishing license!

Winter RV Camping: Essential Tips for Lake Texoma

January 28, 2026 By RV Experts

Winter RV camping at Lake Texoma can be incredibly rewarding with proper preparation. The mild Texas winters make it much more comfortable than northern climates, but you still need to prepare your RV and plan accordingly.

🔧 RV Winterization Checklist

  • Heating System: Service furnace before cold weather arrives
  • Insulation: Add extra insulation to windows and vents
  • Pipe Protection: Insulate water lines and add heat tape if needed
  • Skirting: Install RV skirting to protect underneath
  • Emergency Kit: Extra blankets, flashlights, and batteries

⚡ Managing Power in Cold Weather

  • 50 Amp Advantage: CampPointe's 50 amp service handles multiple heating sources
  • Backup Heat: Electric heaters as backup to propane furnace
  • Energy Efficient: LED lights use less power for heating budget
  • Monitor Usage: Watch power consumption during peak cold

❄️ Freezing Weather Protocol

  • Water Lines: Let faucets drip during freezing nights
  • Hose Disconnect: Disconnect and drain water hose when temperatures drop
  • Tank Heating: Use tank heaters if staying in severe cold
  • Emergency Water: Keep bottled water as backup

🌡️ Staying Comfortable

  • Layer Clothing: Dress in layers inside the RV
  • Warm Bedding: Quality sleeping bags or extra blankets
  • Hot Drinks: Thermos of coffee or hot chocolate for warmth
  • Activity Planning: Plan indoor activities for very cold days
