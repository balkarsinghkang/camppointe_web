🛣️ Directions from Major Cities

From Dallas, TX

~90 miles (1 hour 45 minutes)
  1. Take US-75 North from Dallas
  2. Continue on US-75 North for about 80 miles
  3. Take Exit 69 for FM-901 toward Gordonville
  4. Turn right (east) on FM-901
  5. Continue for 8 miles to CampPointe on the right

Note: Well-marked exits, RV-friendly route with wide lanes

From Oklahoma City, OK

~120 miles (2 hours 15 minutes)
  1. Take I-35 South from Oklahoma City
  2. Continue south for about 90 miles
  3. Take Exit 1C for US-377 South toward Whitesboro
  4. Follow US-377 South for 15 miles
  5. Turn left on FM-901 East
  6. Continue 12 miles to CampPointe

Note: Interstate highway most of the way, easy RV access

From Fort Worth, TX

~95 miles (1 hour 50 minutes)
  1. Take I-35W North from Fort Worth
  2. Merge onto I-35E North
  3. Continue to US-75 North
  4. Follow US-75 North for 60 miles
  5. Take Exit 69 for FM-901
  6. Turn right and continue 8 miles to park

Note: Major highways entire route, big rig accessible

From Austin, TX

~200 miles (3 hours 30 minutes)
  1. Take I-35 North from Austin
  2. Continue north through Dallas area
  3. Merge onto US-75 North
  4. Take Exit 69 for FM-901
  5. Turn right on FM-901 East
  6. Continue 8 miles to CampPointe

Note: Longest drive but straightforward interstate route

From Tulsa, OK

~160 miles (2 hours 45 minutes)
  1. Take US-75 South from Tulsa
  2. Continue south for about 130 miles
  3. Merge with US-69 South
  4. Take US-377 South toward Whitesboro
  5. Turn left on FM-901 East
  6. Continue 12 miles to park entrance

Note: Scenic route through Oklahoma hills

From Houston, TX

~290 miles (4 hours 45 minutes)
  1. Take I-45 North from Houston
  2. Merge onto I-35E North in Dallas
  3. Continue to US-75 North
  4. Take Exit 69 for FM-901
  5. Turn right on FM-901 East
  6. Continue 8 miles to CampPointe

Note: Long haul but excellent highway conditions

🚐 RV-Specific Routing Tips

🛣️

Recommended Routes

Best highways and routes for safe RV travel to CampPointe.

  • Best Overall: US-75 North - widest lanes, best maintained
  • Avoid: Small farm roads and narrow state highways
  • Gas Stations: Multiple RV-friendly fuel stops on US-75
  • Rest Areas: Several clean rest stops along major routes
⚠️

Important Notes

Key information about clearances, restrictions, and conditions.

  • Bridge Clearances: All routes have 13'6"+ clearance
  • Weight Limits: No weight restrictions on major highways
  • Construction: Check TxDOT website for road conditions
  • Weather: Winter ice possible - check conditions
⛽

Fuel & Services

RV-friendly fuel stations and propane refill locations nearby.

  • Love's Travel Stop: Sherman, TX (12 mi) - RV lanes
  • Pilot Flying J: Gainesville, TX (25 mi) - Full service
  • Local Fuel: Several gas stations in Gordonville
  • Propane: Available at hardware stores in Sherman
🏪

Last-Minute Supplies

Convenient stores for groceries and supplies before arrival.

  • Walmart Supercenter: Sherman, TX (20 minutes)
  • Brookshire's Grocery: Sherman, TX (15 minutes)
  • Dollar General: Gordonville, TX (5 minutes)
  • Hardware Store: Local hardware in Gordonville

🏞️ About the Gordonville & Lake Texoma Area

CampPointe is located in Gordonville, Texas, a small rural community on the shores of beautiful Lake Texoma. This area offers the perfect combination of peaceful countryside and exciting water recreation.

Local Highlights

🌊

Lake Texoma

One of the most popular recreational lakes in the South.

  • 89,000-acre reservoir
  • Texas-Oklahoma border
  • Famous for striped bass fishing
  • Premier water sports destination
🏘️

Gordonville

Charming rural community with friendly locals and peaceful atmosphere.

  • Population: 400 people
  • Small-town hospitality
  • Proximity to outdoor recreation
  • Quiet, relaxing environment
🌡️

Climate

Moderate four-season climate ideal for year-round activities.

  • Hot summers for water activities
  • Mild winters for fishing
  • Spectacular spring wildflowers
  • Beautiful fall foliage
📍

Geography

Rolling hills in the scenic cross timbers region of North Texas.

  • Grayson County, Texas
  • Cross timbers region
  • Mix of farmland and forests
  • Elevation: ~650 feet

🌤️ Weather & Best Times to Visit

🌸

Spring (March - May)

Mild conditions with blooming wildflowers, perfect for outdoor activities.

  • Avg Temp: 60-80°F
  • Conditions: Mild, occasional rain
  • Best For: Wildflowers, fishing, hiking
  • Bring: Light jacket, rain gear
☀️

Summer (June - August)

Hot summer weather ideal for water activities and lake recreation.

  • Avg Temp: 75-95°F
  • Conditions: Hot, humid, occasional storms
  • Best For: Swimming, boating, water sports
  • Bring: Sunscreen, lots of water
🍂

Fall (September - November)

Cool and beautiful conditions, perfect for peaceful outdoor adventures.

  • Avg Temp: 55-75°F
  • Conditions: Cool, dry, beautiful
  • Best For: Fishing, hiking, sightseeing
  • Bring: Layers, comfortable clothes
❄️

Winter (December - February)

Mild winters with quiet atmosphere, great for relaxation and fishing.

  • Avg Temp: 35-55°F
  • Conditions: Cool, occasional freezing
  • Best For: Fishing, peaceful relaxation
  • Bring: Warm clothes, heater
