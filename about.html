Our Story

CampPointe Lake Texoma RV Park has been welcoming families and outdoor enthusiasts to the beautiful shores of Lake Texoma. Located in the heart of Texas on the Texas-Oklahoma border, our park offers the perfect blend of natural beauty and modern convenience.

What started as a vision to create the premier RV camping destination at Lake Texoma has grown into a beloved retreat where countless families have created lasting memories. Our commitment to excellence in facilities, service, and natural preservation has made us a favorite among RV travelers from across the region.

We understand that RV camping is more than just a place to park - it's about the experiences, the connections with nature, and the memories made with loved ones. That's why every aspect of CampPointe is designed with our guests' comfort and enjoyment in mind.

The Lake Texoma Region

Lake Texoma is one of the largest reservoirs in the United States, created by the Denison Dam on the Red River. This magnificent body of water straddles the Texas-Oklahoma border and offers some of the best recreational opportunities in the region.

Lake Texoma Features:

  • 89,000 acres of pristine water
  • 580 miles of beautiful, diverse shoreline
  • Excellent fishing opportunities (striped bass, catfish, crappie, bass)
  • World-class water sports and boating
  • Scenic hiking trails and nature areas
  • Wildlife viewing and bird watching
  • Historic sites and cultural attractions nearby

The lake's unique ecosystem supports a diverse range of fish species, making it a premier fishing destination. The clear, clean waters are perfect for swimming, skiing, and all water sports, while the surrounding areas offer hiking, camping, and nature study opportunities.
