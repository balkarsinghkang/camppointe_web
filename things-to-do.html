🎯 Key Attractions

🎣

Lake Texoma Fishing

World-class striped bass fishing destination, often called the "Striper Capital of the South." Exceptional fishing year-round.

  • 89,000-acre reservoir with clear waters
  • Hybrid striped bass tournaments
  • Professional fishing guides available
  • Multiple fishing access points nearby
⛵

Eisenhower State Park

Texas State Park with hiking trails and beach access. Perfect for families and outdoor enthusiasts.

  • 15 minutes from CampPointe
  • Beach areas and swimming
  • Hiking and nature trails
  • Boat ramps and fishing piers
🏛️

Hagerman National Wildlife Refuge

11,000-acre wildlife preserve with diverse ecosystems. Bird watching paradise with 300+ species.

  • 25 minutes from CampPointe
  • Wildlife photography opportunities
  • Educational visitor center
  • Driving and walking trails
🏊

Grandpappy Point Marina

Full-service marina with rentals and services. Your gateway to Lake Texoma water activities.

  • 10 minutes from CampPointe
  • Boat rentals and slips
  • Fuel and marine supplies
  • Restaurant and snack bar
🎨

Denison Main Street

Historic downtown with boutique shopping, antiques, and local dining. Charming small-town atmosphere.

  • 20 minutes from CampPointe
  • Unique shops and boutiques
  • Local restaurants and cafes
  • Art galleries and museums
🏛️

Sherman Museum District

Multiple museums showcasing regional history, art, and culture. Educational and entertaining for all ages.

  • 25 minutes from CampPointe
  • Red River Historical Museum
  • Sherman Museum & Art Gallery
  • Special exhibits throughout year
🍷

Local Wineries

Texas Hill Country wineries offering tastings and tours. Enjoy local wines in beautiful settings.

  • Multiple wineries within 30 minutes
  • Wine tastings and tours
  • Events and live music
  • Beautiful vineyard views
⛳

Golf Courses

Several championship golf courses near Lake Texoma. Scenic layouts with excellent facilities.

  • Tanglewood Resort Championship Course
  • Grayson County College Course
  • Pro shops and club rentals
  • All skill levels welcome

🌊 Water Activities

🎣

Fishing

World-renowned striped bass fishing. Lake Texoma holds multiple state records and hosts professional tournaments.

  • Striped bass (hybrid and native)
  • Largemouth and smallmouth bass
  • Catfish, crappie, and sand bass
  • Year-round fishing opportunities
🚤

Boating

89,000 acres of open water perfect for all types of boating. Multiple marinas provide full services.

  • Powerboat and sailboat friendly
  • Water skiing and wakeboarding
  • Multiple boat ramps
  • Fuel and service available
🏊

Swimming

Clean, clear water with designated swimming areas. Beach access at state parks and marinas.

  • Sandy beach areas
  • Designated swimming zones
  • Lifeguard supervision at state parks
  • Family-friendly areas
🛶

Kayaking & Canoeing

Explore quiet coves and tributaries. Rentals available at multiple locations around the lake.

  • Calm water areas for beginners
  • Wildlife viewing opportunities
  • Rental equipment available
  • Guided tours offered
🏄

Water Sports

Ideal conditions for water skiing, wakeboarding, jet skiing, and other adrenaline-pumping activities.

  • Water skiing and wakeboarding
  • Jet ski rentals available
  • Tubing and parasailing
  • Water sport equipment rentals
⚓

Scuba Diving

Clear water and interesting underwater features make Lake Texoma a popular diving destination.

  • Good visibility most of the year
  • Interesting underwater structures
  • Dive shops in nearby cities
  • Training courses available
